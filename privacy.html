# Neo Sudoku: Project Status and Session Handoff **Updated:** 23 Oct 2026 **Live build:** https://claude.ai/artifact/YFMrxNt81KXeZXFygfNvv8 (single self-contained HTML file) **Stack:** plain HTML/CSS/JS, no build step. Android app planned via Capacitor wrapper (not started). **Abandoned:** the earlier Jetpack Compose project (neo-sudoku.zip). Do not resume it. ## Implemented (all published) - Classic Sudoku, unique-solution puzzles, 4 difficulties graded by human techniques (naked singles, hidden singles, pairs/locked candidates, advanced). - Killer Sudoku: cages with sums, unique-solution generator, cage highlight, cage info strip (sum, cell count, "need N"). - Home screen: per-mode Continue cards (Classic and Killer saved separately), Daily Challenge with streak, expanding Classic/Killer tiles. - Unlocks: Hard needs 10 solved Medium, Expert needs 10 solved Hard, per mode. Daily solves do not count. Tap the title 5 times for test mode (unlock all). - Game: notes, undo, erase, 3 hints, 3 lives, same-number and peer highlight, auto-save/resume, best times, floating pill bar. - Themes (user decisions 21 Oct): exactly three switches in Look & sound: Style (Classic / Neumorphic / Neon), Mode (Light / Dark; with Neon only Dark is shown), Colours (Colourful / Single colour; single colour opens the 10-accent picker of floating circles). Neon = true black everywhere (page, panels, cells, cards, sheet, bar; cells are faint outlines in their box colour); Neon app chrome (user reference 21 Oct, black/lime ticket-app style): pitch-black page, flat near-black tiles for cards, number pad, bar and sheets, and the fluorescent accent used as solid fills with black text (top Continue card, main buttons, selected options, active switch segments, switches when on, streak and hint badges); no glowing edges. Selection in every style (user request 21 Oct): every cell in the selected row, column and box gets its own edge in the selection colour (box colour when colourful, else the accent): a deeper shade in Light, a soft glow in Dark, around the raised tile in Neumorphic; the selected cell and matching numbers fill with that colour with a readable digit; Killer's selected cage stays a fill. Neon + colourful also colours the app chrome with the fluorescent palette (Continue card, progress bars, streak, icons, switch segments, buttons). Neon grid (user picked mockup A, 21 Oct): plain dark cells with white numbers; the selected cell and matching numbers fill with the selection colour (selected cell's box colour when colourful, else the accent) and their number turns black; every cell in the selected row, column and box gets a glowing edge. Neon Killer: cages are faint grey dashed outlines; the selected cage is a soft fill of the selection colour inside its cells, so it never overlaps the glowing edges. Rejected for Neon: fluorescent tiles, per-cell rings, glowing cage tubes, strip outlines (intersecting lines); switches/buttons get thin glowing accent edges. Also neon-tube box outlines (halo + faint inner glow) in each box's colour (colourful) or the accent (single); your digits glow and take their box colour when colourful; givens stay white; selected cell is a bright fill with a glow; bar, home cards and number pad get glowing accent edges. Classic Dark and Neumorphic Dark follow the user's dark-neumorphism reference (21 Oct): a fixed cool slate base (hue 222, independent of the accent) kept close to black (backgrounds 5-7% lightness, user request 21 Oct), off-white text, deep black shadows; Neumorphic adds a soft sheen on raised surfaces; the accent is a two-tone gradient (accent into its neighbouring hue) on the selected cell and main buttons. Never tint dark surfaces with the accent. System mode and the old glow switch are gone; old saved choices migrate (AMOLED -> Neon). Switches, swatches and sound/haptics switches are neumorphic and animated in every style. - Win celebration (user picked option B from mockups 21 Oct): a card springs up and fills up to 3 stars. Stars = lower of a time score and a hint score (no hints = 3, one = 2, more = 1); time targets per mode and difficulty in `PAR`. Shows title, time, "New best by" and unlock badges, a tip for the next star, Home / Next puzzle (dailies: Another day). Star dings, confetti, haptics. The old full-grid spin on win was removed. - Grid (all styles, user request 21 Oct): each 3x3 box is a separate rounded panel with a clear gap; cells are equal square tiles. Colourful gives each box its own pastel; single colour uses one accent-tinted panel colour. - Killer cages (user picked from mockups 21 Oct): each cage is a soft tint (6 fixed hues, neighbouring cages always differ); no dashed lines. Only the selected cage gets a gold outline. Row/column highlight is an overlay so cage tints stay visible. - 3D celebrations: row/column flip, box flip, full-puzzle grid spin plus 3D confetti. - Smart hints (Classic): naked/hidden single, pointing pair/triple, box-line reduction, naked/hidden pair; first tap names the technique (costs a hint), "Show answer" is free. Killer hints only highlight a cell and its cage. Auto-notes button in the bar (5 bar slots: Undo, Erase, Notes, Auto, Hint). - Daily Challenge calendar: per-date puzzles (difficulty by weekday), solved dates highlighted, backfill allowed, streak counts only same-day solves. Daily saves are per date (`daily-YYYY-MM-DD`). - Monthly coins (user rules 21 Oct): a month's coin is earned when every day of it is solved; backfilled days count; future days are locked, so the current month can only complete on its last day. Each month has its own coloured coin (`coinSVG`, hues in `MHUE`): greyed dashed outline until earned, shown beside the month name on the calendar with progress text; when a daily solve completes a month the coin flips in on the win card (`COINNEW`, set in `dailyDone`); earned coins are collected on the stats screen. Derived from `DS.done` (`fullMonths`), nothing extra is stored. - Stats screen (user picked bento design C, 21 Oct): solid streak hero (selection colour, readable text) with last-7-day dots, win-rate ring, stars earned (+ average), puzzles won (+ dailies, lost), time played (+ fastest), next-unlock progress, 8 achievements derived from history, best times. Themed in every style (in Glass the hero is clear glass with theme-coloured text, 29 Oct); numbers count up, ring and bar animate (instant with reduced motion); no gradients. Stars, rated games and three-star solves are counted from 21 Oct onward (`ST.stars`, `ST.rated`, `ST.three`). Scrollbars are hidden app-wide. Slide transitions between home, game and stats. - Sound effects (synthesized Web Audio, no files) with Sound and Haptics switches in the Look & sound sheet. ## Android app (started 22 Oct, details in docs/ANDROID.md) - Capacitor 8 project in `android/` (plugins: App, Haptics, SplashScreen, @capacitor-community/admob).
