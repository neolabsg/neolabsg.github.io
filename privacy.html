Neo Sudoku Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Neo Sudoku app for Android ("the app") uses, why, and the choices you have. The app is made by [DEVELOPER NAME] ("we", "us").

The short version

  • You don't need an account. We don't ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos.
  • Your games, settings, statistics, goals and medals are saved only on your phone. We never receive them.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device information to show and measure those ads, as described below.
  • If you buy "Remove ads", Google Play handles the payment. We never see your card or payment details.

Information stored on your phone

The app saves your puzzles in progress, settings (such as theme and sound), statistics, daily streak, goals and medals in the app's private storage on your phone. This information is not sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in your phone's settings, deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the app uses Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC, to show a full-screen ad after a finished puzzle and an optional ad you can watch for an extra hint. To do this, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and processes information from your device, such as:

  • your device's advertising ID, and device and app information (for example device model, operating system version and app version);
  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location (country or region);
  • how you interact with ads (for example whether an ad was shown or clicked), and diagnostic information about ad performance.

Google uses this information to deliver, measure and improve ads, to prevent fraud and, where you allow it, to personalise ads. We do not receive this information in a form that identifies you. For details, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your consent and choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: before ads are personalised, the app shows Google's consent message, where you can accept, refuse or choose your options. You can change your choice at any time from the app's Settings.
  • Personalised ads: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's settings (on most phones: Settings, Google, Ads, or Settings, Privacy, Ads).
  • No ads: the one-time "Remove ads" purchase turns off all ads in the app.

In-app purchase

"Remove ads" is sold through Google Play's billing system. Google processes the payment under its own terms and privacy policy. The app only learns whether the purchase was made, so it can turn ads off and restore the purchase if you reinstall.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from the advertising and payment processing by Google described above, the app does not share information with third parties.

Security and retention

Your game data stays on your phone and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data. Information collected by Google for ads is kept according to Google's own retention policies.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. Significant changes will be noted in the app's store listing.
